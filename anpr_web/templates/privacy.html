Privacy

Data handling in this prototype

Vehicle and visit records

The public registry never displays email addresses. Authenticated administrators can manage fictional vehicle records and optional notification consent. Visit history retains timestamps and fictional registry details for demonstration reporting.

Email consent and retention

Visit-summary email is opt-in and requires a valid address. It is sent only after a completed exit when delivery is configured. Delivery records retain a masked destination and status, never message bodies, credentials, or full provider responses. Removing or deactivating a vehicle prevents new summary messages.

Images

Uploaded and browser-camera stills are validated, processed in temporary storage, and deleted. Review images are embedded in the immediate response and are not persisted by the application.

Prototype limitation

This access-decision prototype is not production-ready and does not control a gate or physical barrier. Use synthetic or explicitly consented data only.
